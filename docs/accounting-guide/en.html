Standards

The rules

From NSBU to NSFO

Accounting follows Law ZRU-404 on Accounting. A company with foreign capital follows the same rules as a local one.

  1. 1 Jan 2025New chart of accounts and forms
  2. 17 Dec 2025Full IFRS only for public-interest entities
  3. 2026Last year on NSBU
  4. 1 Jan 2027NSFO No. 1 and No. 2, based on IFRS for SMEs
  5. 1 Mar 2028First statements under NSFO

In practice

Only NSFO No. 1 and No. 2 are adopted so far. Fixed assets, inventories and revenue stay on NSBU until new standards replace them.

Books and policy

One chart of accounts, one policy order

Since 2025 every company keeps its books on one national chart of accounts. An ERP on the group chart needs a mapping to it.

  1. Registration
    The policy applies from day one
  2. Within 90 days
    The director signs the policy order
  3. From 1 January
    Changes take effect
  4. By 31 Dec 2026
    Sign the policy for 2027

People and documents

Who keeps the books

The director picks one of four models the law allows. Whichever it is, every sale gets an e-invoice and documents are kept for at least five years.

  • Own team led by a chief accountant
  • An accountant under a contract
  • Outsourcing to a specialised firm
  • The director keeps the books

In practice

The work moves, the responsibility does not. The company and its director answer to the state.
