Standards

The rules

From NSBU to NSFO in one timeline

Accounting follows Law ZRU-404 on Accounting. A company with foreign capital follows the same rules as a local one.

1 Jan 2025
New chart of accounts and forms
17 Dec 2025
Full IFRS only for public-interest entities
2026
Last year on NSBU
1 Jan 2027
NSFO No. 1 and No. 2Based on IFRS for SMEs
1 Mar 2028
First statements under NSFO

What stays in 2027

  • The other NSBU, until new standards replace them
  • The national chart of accounts, books in soums and the deadlines

What to watch

  • Further NSFO are in preparation
  • A new accounting law exists only as a draft
Common mistake

Expecting a whole new rulebook

Only NSFO No. 1 and No. 2 are adopted, so fixed assets, inventories and revenue stay on NSBU for now.
