Special regimes

Main incentives for investors

  • Special economic zones: profit tax exemption for 3 to 10 years depending on investment, plus property and land tax exemptions. Participants from 1 April 2026 get accelerated depreciation instead (Art. 473)
  • Companies with foreign direct investment: property and land tax exemptions depending on the amount invested (Arts 471 and 472)
  • IT Park: residents exempt from taxes and social tax, 7.5% personal income tax on salaries (Resolution PP-589). From 2028 to 2040, all taxes except VAT (Art. 483)
  • Creative Park: personal income tax on salaries and social tax at rates reduced by 50% until 1 January 2031 (Art. 483)

Rates as of November 2026 under the Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan and the IT Park regulations. Incentives, special regimes and tax treaties apply only when their conditions are met.
