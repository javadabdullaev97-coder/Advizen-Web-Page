Rules

The basics

Two kinds of operations

The Currency Regulation Law ZRU-573 of 2019 splits cross-border operations in two. The Central Bank sets the detailed rules.

Current operations

  • Goods, works and services
  • Interest and dividends
  • Royalties and licence fees
  • Salaries

FreeIncluding the purchase of currency for them

Capital operations

  • Loans and leasing
  • Investments
  • Real estate
  • Accounts and deposits abroad

Separate rulesForeign direct investment and its repatriation are free

Not completed within 180 days

In practice

An Uzbek subsidiary is a resident and follows these rules in full. A branch or representative office of a foreign company is a non-resident with its own account rules.

What changed in 2026

The law stayed, the procedures got faster. Most changes came from Central Bank rules and Cabinet resolutions.

  1. Jan 2026The foreign trade system is renamed E-kontrakt
  2. Apr 2026Banks decide on currency orders by the next banking day
  3. Jul 2026Bought currency can change its purpose
  4. Sep 2026Parent loans are registered within one working day
  5. 1 Jan 2027An identification code for every non-resident in E-kontrakt

Trade

E-kontrakt

The path of a payment abroad

Every foreign trade contract or invoice goes into E-kontrakt before the first operation, services included. Without the record the bank pays no advance and customs release no goods.

  1. Contract in E-kontrakt
    Before the first operation. Changes only by a supplementary agreement
  2. Bank check
    Before an advance or a letter of credit the bank checks the record
  3. Currency purchase
    An order with its purpose, decided by the next banking day
  4. Payment
    From the company's own account in an Uzbek bank
  5. Act or customs declaration
    Customs check goods against the record. Service acts go in by the 20th of the next month

E-kontrakt is the system of the Customs Committee, called EEISVO until 2026. The rules are set by Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 283.

Repatriation

180 days to close a deal

The foreign party has 180 days to deliver the goods or services, pay or return the advance. After that a fine on the unreturned amount grows in steps (Law ZRU-573, Art. 11-1).

  1. Day 0Payment, export or signed act
  2. Day 180The deal counts as a capital operation
  3. Day 225Fine of 5%Day 270 for small businesses
  4. Day 360Plus 10%
  5. Day 545Plus 35%, up to 50% in total
  • Day 0 is the payment for imports, customs clearance for exports and the signed act for services
  • Set-off, novation or an insurance payment also close the deal
  • No fine while overdue export receivables stay within 10% of export proceeds over 36 months

The tax authority sends a demand and a court imposes the fine. From 2027 a presidential decree lets courts halve it. Amendments to the law are under review.

Accounts and conversion

Seven banking days to spend currency

Currency is bought and sold only through Uzbek banks. There is no limit on buying currency for current operations, and the mandatory sale of export proceeds ended in 2017.

  1. Main account
    The first soum account is the main oneThe bank opens it by the next working day after a full set of documents
  2. Order
    Purpose and supporting documentsThe bank decides and settles by the next banking day
  3. Special account
    Bought currency serves only its purposeSince July 2026 the purpose can switch to another current operation
  4. 7 banking days
    Unused currency goes backThe bank sells it at that day's market rate without the client's consent

An account abroad needs a decision of the President or the Government or an international treaty, and quarterly reports.

Inside the country

Soums only, and less cash

Inside Uzbekistan prices and payments are in soums only (Law ZRU-573, Art. 9). Pegging a price to the dollar is not allowed, and salaries are paid in soums too.

  • Purchases above 400 BCV are paid only by card or electronically
  • Since July 2026 trade and service companies must accept the single QR code
  • A soum corporate card covers expenses in Uzbekistan, a currency card covers business trips abroad
  • Ads on foreign social networks may be paid by card, up to USD 5,000 a month

From 25 January 2027 domestic card payments in soums run through the Central Bank's national payment switch.

Money with the parent

In and out of the subsidiary

The Investment Law ZRU-598 guarantees a foreign investor free transfers abroad once taxes are paid.

FlowRule
Contribution to capitalInto UzbekistanFree. Capital is set in soums and paid in currency or property within a year of registration
Loan from the parentInto UzbekistanRegistered with the Central Bank through the bank before the money arrives
Interest, services and royaltiesOut of UzbekistanFree current payments. Service contracts go through E-kontrakt
DividendsOut of UzbekistanFree after taxes. An LLC pays them within 60 days of the decision
Return of capitalOut of UzbekistanThrough a capital reduction or an exit, after creditors are settled

Bank checks

Who stands behind the company

A bank must know who owns and runs its client. If it cannot complete the check, it may refuse the operation.

  • Owners are traced to every individual with 10% or more, below the usual 25%
  • The bank must understand the business and the ownership structure
  • Large and unusual operations get a closer look
  • Politically exposed persons and high-risk jurisdictions get enhanced checks

In practice

Have an ownership chart down to every individual with 10% or more ready before you open the account.
