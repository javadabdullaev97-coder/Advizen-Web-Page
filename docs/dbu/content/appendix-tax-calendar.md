title: Tax Calendar
lead: The main filing and payment deadlines for a company on the general tax regime, month by month and year by year. Payment is due no later than the filing date unless stated otherwise.
---

## Monthly deadlines

Most taxes are reported every month. When a deadline falls on a weekend or public holiday, it moves to the next working day.

:::table Every month
| Day | Tax | What is due |
|---|---|---|
| 10th | Excise | Return and payment for the previous month |
| 10th | Property and land tax | Advance of one twelfth of the annual tax |
| 15th | Personal income tax and social tax | Return and payment for the previous month |
| 15th | Turnover tax | Return and payment for the previous month |
| 20th | VAT | Return and payment for the previous month |
| 20th | Withholding tax on non-residents | Return for the previous month |
| 20th | Water use tax | Monthly advance for larger payers |
| 20th | Subsoil use tax | Cumulative return and payment |
| 23rd | Corporate income tax | Monthly advance, if prior-year income exceeded UZS 20 billion |
:::

Withholding tax on payments to non-residents is paid no later than the day after the payment. Tax on dividends paid to residents is paid no later than the dividend itself. In January, property and land tax advances are due by 20 January. Businesses on the simplified 6% VAT file a separate monthly VAT return instead of the standard one.

## Quarterly deadlines

:::table Every quarter
| Date | Tax | What is due |
|---|---|---|
| 20 April, July, October | Corporate income tax | Quarterly return and payment |
| 20th after each quarter | VAT on foreign digital services | Return and payment by the foreign provider |
| 20 March, June, September, December | Property, land and water tax | Quarterly advance for turnover tax payers |
:::

## Annual deadlines

:::table Every year
| Date | Tax or report | What is due |
|---|---|---|
| 20 January | Property and water tax | Estimate of the year's tax for advances |
| 20 January | Land tax, non-agricultural land | Annual return |
| 15 February | Personal income tax and social tax | Annual return of the employer |
| 15 February | Turnover tax | Annual return |
| 1 March | Corporate income tax | Annual return and final payment |
| 1 March | Property tax and water use tax | Annual return and final payment |
| 1 March | Financial statements under national standards | Annual statements; micro and small firms: balance sheet and income statement |
| 1 March | Transfer pricing | Notification of controlled transactions |
| 1 March | Voluntary transition to IFRS | Notice to the tax authority (PP-282, para 6(a)) |
| 20 March | Controlled foreign companies | Notification for the previous year |
| 1 April | Personal income tax | Individual declaration, with payment by 1 June |
| 1 May | Financial statements under IFRS | Annual statements |
| 1 May | Land tax, agricultural land | Annual return |
| 15 June | Statutory audit | Copy of the audit report to the tax authority |
| 15 June | Public-interest entities | Publication of audited IFRS statements |
:::

Controlled transactions are notified by the deadline for the annual financial statements, so a company reporting under IFRS notifies by 1 May. A new participation in a foreign company must be notified within one month. Joint-stock companies publish audited annual statements at least two weeks before the annual general meeting, which is held within six months of year end (Law on Accounting, Art. 26; JSC Law, Arts. 58, 102). There is no quarterly financial reporting.
