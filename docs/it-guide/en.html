Why Uzbekistan

A growing base for IT teams

A large young workforce, a time zone between Europe and Asia and one of the most generous tax regimes for IT companies in the region.

Software development, outsourcing and IT services are among the fastest growing exports of the country. Many foreign companies use Tashkent as a delivery centre for clients in Europe, the Middle East and the CIS.

The IT Park is the main instrument. Its residents are exempt from most taxes, their employees pay a reduced personal income tax, and they can hire foreign specialists without work permits.

IT Park

Becoming a resident

The IT Park (Technological Park of Software Products and Information Technologies) admits Uzbek legal entities that carry on IT activities from its official list.

Who can apply

  • Uzbek legal entities only. A foreign company first registers a local LLC
  • Software development and IT services
  • IT outsourcing and business process outsourcing
  • Data processing and other activities on the list in Resolution PP-589

Who cannot

  • Since 1 April 2026, payment organisations and payment system operators
  • Marketplaces
  • Microfinance organisations

These activities lost access to the benefits under Presidential Resolution PP-388 of 26 December 2025.

From registration to resident status

  1. Register an LLCState registration of the Uzbek company2 to 3 days
  2. File the applicationApplication and business plan, filed online
  3. ReviewBy the IT Park directorateUp to 15 working days
  4. Resident statusAgreement with the IT Park and entry in the register of residents

Benefits apply from the first day of the month after the company is entered in the register of residents.

IT Park

Keeping the status

Resolution PP-388 tightened the regime from 1 April 2026. Residents now have to earn part of their income from exports and pay a monthly contribution to the park.

20%Minimum export share of total income from 1 April 2026
35%Minimum export share of total income from 1 January 2027
1%Monthly contribution, as a share of revenue, when the export share is met

If the export share is not met

The contribution rises to 2% of revenue at UZS 5 to 100 billion of revenue and to 3% above UZS 100 billion.

From 2028 to 2040

The exemption from all taxes except VAT continues to 1 January 2040, but only for residents whose exports exceed 50% of total income (Tax Code, Art. 483).

In practice

  • Plan the share of export contracts before applying, not after
  • Keep the contracts, invoices and payments that prove export income
  • Track the export share every month and report to the IT Park on time

Relocation

Moving people to Uzbekistan

Entry and IT visa

  • Visa-free entry: citizens of most CIS countries without a time limit, citizens of the EU, the UK, the US, China, Korea, Japan and the Gulf states for 30 days
  • E-visa online for many other nationalities
  • IT visa: multiple entry for up to three years, on the recommendation of the IT Park, for IT specialists, investors, founders and managers of residents. Renewable without leaving the country

Work and registration

  • No work permit for foreign specialists employed by IT Park residents
  • PINFL, the personal identification number, for each foreign employee
  • Registration of stay within three working days of entry
  • Other employers need a work permit for each foreign employee: up to three years for qualified and highly qualified specialists, up to one year for others

Families

Plan visas and registration for spouses and children together with the employee. The IT Park exemption from work permits covers the specialist, not family members.

Hiring and payroll

Employing the team

Labour Code essentials

  • Written contract, on paper or electronic, registered in the Unified National Labour System
  • Probation of up to three months
  • 40-hour week, at least 21 calendar days of annual leave
  • Remote work is allowed and set out in the contract
  • An employee resigns with 14 calendar days' notice
  • Dismissal by the employer only on grounds listed in the Code, with notice and severance

Payroll

  • Salaries are paid in soums, taxes are withheld every month
  • IT Park residents withhold 7.5% until 1 January 2028 and pay no social tax
  • Other employers withhold 12% and pay 12% social tax on top of salary

Banking

  • Accounts in soums and in foreign currency with Uzbek banks
  • Payments between residents inside Uzbekistan in soums, cross-border payments in any currency
  • Accounts with foreign banks only where a law or decision allows it

Before you start

Where time is lost

A few points are worth planning early. We check them for each case before the work starts.

Common pitfalls

  • The apostille of the parent company's documents is often the longest step
  • The company must actually be present at its legal address
  • With foreign founders the bank checks the founders and beneficial owners separately
  • Intellectual property created by employees should be assigned to the company in the contract
  • Founders that do not qualify for resident rates on dividends should check the tax treaty with their country

Main documents

  • Parent company charter and register extract, apostilled and translated
  • Decision to establish the Uzbek company
  • Passport of the director
  • Legal address of the company
  • Business plan and planned export contracts for the IT Park
  • Passports valid for at least six months, and apostilled marriage and birth certificates for families

Requirements can differ by nationality and are updated often. We confirm the exact list for each case.
