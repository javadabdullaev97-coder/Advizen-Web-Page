Why Uzbekistan

A growing base for IT teams

Uzbekistan combines a large young workforce, a time zone between Europe and Asia and one of the most generous tax regimes for IT companies in the region.

Software development, outsourcing and IT services are among the fastest growing exports of the country. Many foreign companies use Tashkent as a delivery centre for clients in Europe, the Middle East and the CIS.

The IT Park is the main instrument. Its residents are exempt from most taxes, their employees pay a reduced personal income tax, and they can hire foreign specialists without work permits.

Entry is simple for many nationalities. Citizens of the CIS, the EU, the UK, the US, China and many other countries enter visa-free, and IT specialists can obtain a multiple-entry IT visa for up to three years.

This guide explains the ways to start, how the IT Park works, what relocating a team involves, and the order in which the steps usually run.

IT Park

Becoming a resident

The IT Park (Technological Park of Software Products and Information Technologies) admits Uzbek legal entities that carry on IT activities from its official list.

Who can apply

  • Uzbek legal entities only. A foreign company first registers a local LLC
  • Software development and IT services
  • IT outsourcing and business process outsourcing
  • Data processing and other activities on the official list in Resolution PP-589

Who cannot

  • Since 1 April 2026, payment organisations and payment system operators
  • Marketplaces
  • Microfinance organisations

These activities lost access to the benefits under Presidential Resolution PP-388 of 26 December 2025.

From registration to resident status

  1. Register an LLCState registration of the Uzbek company2 to 3 days
  2. Prepare the fileApplication, charter and business plan
  3. File the applicationThrough the personal cabinet at my.it-park.uz with the company's digital signature
  4. ReviewThe IT Park directorate reviews the applicationUp to 15 working days
  5. Resident statusAgreement with the IT Park and entry in the register of residents

Benefits apply from the first day of the month after the company is entered in the register of residents.

IT Park

Keeping the status

Resolution PP-388 tightened the regime from 1 April 2026. Residents now have to earn part of their income from exports and pay a monthly contribution to the park.

20%Minimum export share of total income from 1 April 2026
35%Minimum export share of total income from 1 January 2027
1%Monthly contribution to the park, as a share of revenue, for residents that meet the export share

If the export share is not met

The contribution rises to 2% of revenue for residents with revenue of UZS 5 to 100 billion and to 3% above UZS 100 billion.

From 2028 to 2040

The Tax Code continues the exemption from all taxes except VAT from 1 January 2028 to 1 January 2040, but only for residents whose exports exceed 50% of total income (Art. 483).

What this means in practice

  • Plan the share of export contracts before applying, not after
  • Keep contracts, invoices and payment records that prove export income
  • Track the export share every month and the 50% threshold for 2028
  • Report to the IT Park on time to keep the status

Worth knowing

  • Employees' income from a resident is taxed at 7.5% and is not added to their annual income for tax purposes

Relocation

Moving people to Uzbekistan

Entry and IT visa

  • Visa-free entry for citizens of the CIS, the EU, the UK, the US, China and many other countries, mostly for 30 days
  • E-visa online at e-visa.gov.uz for many other nationalities
  • IT visa: multiple entry for up to three years, on the recommendation of the IT Park, for IT specialists, investors, founders and managers of residents
  • The IT visa can be renewed without leaving the country

Work and registration

  • No work permit for foreign specialists employed by IT Park residents
  • PINFL, the personal identification number, for each foreign employee at a Public Services Centre
  • Registration of stay within three working days of entry
  • Employers outside the IT Park need a work permit for each foreign employee. Qualified specialists get permits for up to three years

Families

Plan visas and registration for spouses and children together with the employee. Keep apostilled marriage and birth certificates with notarised translations ready before the move.

Hiring and payroll

Employing the team

Labour Code essentials

  • Written contract, on paper or electronic, registered in the Unified National Labour System
  • Probation of up to three months
  • 40-hour working week and at least 21 calendar days of annual leave
  • Remote work is allowed and must be set out in the contract
  • An employee resigns with 14 calendar days' notice
  • Dismissal by the employer only on grounds listed in the Code, with notice and severance

Payroll

  • Salaries are paid in soums and payroll taxes are withheld every month
  • IT Park residents withhold 7.5% personal income tax and pay no social tax
  • Other employers withhold 12% and pay 12% social tax on top of salary
  • Intellectual property created by employees should be assigned to the company in the contract

Banking and money flows

  • Uzbek companies hold accounts in soums and in foreign currency with Uzbek banks
  • Payments between residents inside Uzbekistan are made in soums, cross-border payments in any currency
  • An Uzbek company may hold accounts with foreign banks only where a law or decision allows it
  • The bank account application can be filed together with state registration. For a company with foreign founders the bank checks the founders and beneficial owners separately
  • The company must actually be present at its legal address

Checklist

IT Park application

Family members
