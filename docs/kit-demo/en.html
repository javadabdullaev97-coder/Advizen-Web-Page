Good to know

Returns arrive pre-filled

Since 2026 the tax authority prepares payroll returns itself. Check them within five days, or they count as filed.

Common mistake

Treating 7.5% as permanent

The IT Park rate on salaries runs until 1 January 2028. Plan payroll costs beyond that date.
