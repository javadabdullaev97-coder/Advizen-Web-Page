# Image prompts — `advizen`

Scenes for `social/posts/advizen.toml`. Frames go to
`public/social/source/advizen/`, which is tracked.

## What three rounds established

**Concrete beats abstract, every time.** The one frame that worked —
`05-consequence.jpg` — held a tax return, a payroll sheet and an
employment contract, nameable objects from the actual work, with a real
paperclip for the crimson. Everything that failed was a metaphor made of
shapes: faceted crystals, glass panels floating in black, stone tablets, a
thread of light. A generator renders those as jewellery.

**Variety comes from different places, not different abstractions.** Four
rooms beat four sculptures. Each prompt below is a different real
interior with depth and furniture, and none of them is a tabletop.

**The crimson is an object, not a glow.** A badge, a folder, a box file,
a bound cover. Light can catch it; it should not be made of light.

## 01 — Cover

Four practices, one firm.

```
A long dark office corridor at night, photographed straight down its
length. Four closed doors lead off it, two on each side, all unlit. From
beneath all four doors at once, the same band of deep oxblood light spills
onto the polished floor. Worn parquet, a radiator, the edge of a fire
door. Soft broad light from a window at the far end, deep shadow
everywhere else, warm and very low-key, near-black. The oxblood light is
deep and desaturated and is the only colour in the frame. Photorealistic
architectural photography, 35mm, shallow depth of field. The corridor and
the doors sit in the lower two thirds; the top third of the frame is dark
and empty. 4:5 vertical, 1620x2025.

Negative prompt: text, letters, numbers, words, signage, nameplate,
readable writing, watermark, logo, face, eyes, portrait, people looking at

yellow, hard rim light, bright white edge highlights, specular glare, 3D
render, illustration, cartoon, faceted crystal, floating glass panels,
abstract sculpture, bright daylight, white walls, empty void background,
tabletop still life.
```

Ten billion in deals, eighty registrations, thirty due diligence reviews.
The quantity is the subject.

```

right, is pulled half out and is deep oxblood; every other box is dark.
Dust in the air, worn shelf edges, a concrete floor. Soft broad light from
a high window, deep shadow, warm and very low-key, near-black. The oxblood

## 06 — Three

Three laws drafted with the team's involvement. The count is in the
picture, so the card's own numeral never competes with a generated one.

```
An empty parliamentary committee room after hours, shot from the back of
the chamber. Curved rows of vacant seats face a low speaker's rostrum. On
the rostrum lie exactly three closed folders in deep oxblood, side by
side, left behind; nothing else is on it. Dark panelled walls, a
microphone stand, one lamp still burning over the rostrum. Soft broad
light, deep shadow, warm and very low-key, near-black. The three folders
are deep and desaturated and are the only colour in the frame.
Photorealistic interior photography, 35mm, shallow depth of field. The
