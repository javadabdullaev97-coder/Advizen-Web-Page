# Image prompts — `advizen`

Scenes for `social/posts/advizen.toml`. Read `README.md` in this folder
first — especially **Rotate the register**. Frames go to
`public/social/source/advizen/`, which is tracked.

Every slide here is in a different register on purpose. An earlier draft
of this file put all six on a desk with a hand and a sheet of paper, and
six near identical frames make no carousel.

---

## 01 — Cover

*Register: diorama.* Four practices, one firm.

```
A dark tabletop shot from a low three-quarter angle, carrying an
architectural model: four separate miniature buildings standing apart, each
a different height, each lit from within. A single thread of deep oxblood
light runs along the surface and links all four to one small central
pavilion. Fine dust, scale-model texture, visible seams in the card and
acrylic. Soft broad light, long quiet shadows, warm and low-key, near-black
surroundings. The oxblood thread is deep and desaturated and is the only
colour in the frame. Photorealistic, shallow depth of field, tilt-shift.
The model sits in the lower two thirds; the top of the frame is dark and
empty. 4:5 vertical, 1620x2025.

Negative prompt: face, eyes, portrait, people looking at camera, text,
letters, numbers, words, readable writing, watermark, logo, signage, bright
red, ruby, scarlet, saturated colours, orange, teal, yellow, hard rim light,
bright white edge highlights, specular glare, low-poly, cartoon,
illustration, bright daylight, white background, desk with papers and a
hand, still life on a table.
```

count is in the picture, so the card's own numeral never competes with a
generated one.

```
A dark stone chamber, empty and quiet. Three tall vertical tablets stand
side by side on a low plinth, carved from dark stone, their surfaces smooth
and unmarked. The middle tablet is lit from behind with a deep oxblood glow
that spills across the floor. Shot from floor level so the tablets rise out
of the top of the frame. Cold air, hard stone, monumental scale. Soft broad
light, long quiet shadows, warm and low-key, near-black surroundings. The
```
