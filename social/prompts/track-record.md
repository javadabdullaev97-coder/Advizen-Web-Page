# Image prompts — `track-record`

Frames go to `public/social/source/track-record/`, which is tracked.

## These are grounds, not scenes

`engagement` lays the figure, its label and the headline across the **foot**
of the frame at full width, and takes the deeper flatten toward
`theme.GROUND_TARGET`. So the rule is the opposite of the one for `scene`:

- **The subject lives in the upper two thirds.** The bottom third goes
  black under the gradient and under the type.
- **Quiet.** One subject, not a busy situation. Anything happening down
  low is lost.

Everything else holds: brief it as **daylight** (the renderer supplies all
the darkness there will be), the **crimson is an object and never a
light**, no text or numerals anywhere in the frame, 4:5 vertical at
1620×2025, photorealistic photography.

One register each, and none repeats — see `README.md`.
